
### **Draw Steel: Combat Rules Summary**

This document outlines the core mechanics for running combat encounters in the Draw Steel roleplaying game.

#### **The Combat Round**

Combat is structured in rounds, with each creature taking one turn per round.

- **Initiative:** Draw Steel uses a side-based initiative system. At the start of combat (after determining surprise), one side is determined to go first (usually via a d10 roll). Turns then alternate between the heroes' side and the Director's side. The players decide amongst themselves which hero acts when it is their side's turn.
    
- **Surprise:** Creatures who are unaware of their enemies at the start of combat are **surprised**. A surprised creature cannot take triggered actions, and ability rolls made against them gain an **edge**. Surprise ends at the end of the first combat round.
    

#### **Actions on a Turn**

On your turn, you can perform one of each of the following actions in any order. You can break up your movement between your other actions.

- **Main Action:** Your most significant action. This is typically used to activate a powerful class ability or make a concerted attack.
    
- **Maneuver:** A smaller, quicker action. This includes things like grabbing an enemy, aiding an ally, or using certain class features.
    
- **Move Action:** Used to move around the battlefield.
    
- **Action Flexibility:** You can choose to use your Main Action to instead perform an additional Maneuver or an additional Move Action.
    

- **Triggered Action:** An action used in response to a specific trigger (e.g., "when an enemy deals damage to you"). You can only use **one** triggered action per round.
    
- **Free Triggered Action:** Functions like a triggered action, but does not count against your one-per-round limit.
    
- **Free Maneuver:** A very simple action that doesn't cost a maneuver, such as drawing a weapon or opening an unlocked door. You can perform these freely on your turn.
    
- **No Action:** The simplest activities, like shouting a brief warning or dropping an item, which can be done at any time.
    

#### **Movement Rules**

- **Speed:** Your speed is the number of squares you can move when you take the Advance move action.
    
- **Moving Through Spaces:** You can move freely through an ally's space. An enemy's space is considered **difficult terrain** (costs 1 additional square of movement to enter). You cannot end your move in another creature's space unless they are two or more sizes larger or smaller than you.
    
- **Shifting:** A careful, deliberate movement that does **not** provoke opportunity attacks. Certain abilities allow you to shift. You cannot shift into or out of difficult terrain.
    
- **Climbing & Swimming:** Unless you have a feature that allows you to do so automatically, it costs 2 squares of movement to climb or swim 1 square. A test may be required for difficult surfaces or rough water.
    
- **Flying:** Allows movement through the air. If a flying creature is knocked prone or has its speed reduced to 0, it falls. Creatures with the **hover** ability ignore this.
    
- **Jumping:** As part of a move, you can automatically long jump a number of squares up to your Might or Agility score. A test is required to jump farther or higher.
    
- **Teleporting:** Instantaneous movement that bypasses obstacles and does not provoke opportunity attacks. You must have line of effect to the destination space. Teleporting ends the **grabbed** and **restrained** conditions.
    

#### **Falling Rules**

- **Damage:** A creature takes **2 damage for each square they fall** after the first, up to a maximum of 50 damage. They land **prone**.
    
- **Reducing Damage:** A creature can reduce the effective distance of the fall by a number of squares equal to their **Agility score**.
    
- **Falling on a Creature:** If a creature falls onto another, both creatures take the fall damage. The falling creature lands prone in the nearest unoccupied space. If the falling creature's size is greater than the landed-upon creature's Might score, the landed-upon creature is also knocked prone.
    

#### **Key Combat Concepts**

- **Stamina:** This represents a creature's health and vitality. Damage reduces Stamina.
    
- **Winded:** A creature is winded when their Stamina is at or below half of their maximum. This state can trigger certain abilities.
    
- **Dying:** When a creature's Stamina is 0 or lower, they are **dying**. A dying creature can still act but is **bleeding** and cannot use the Catch Breath maneuver. They die if their Stamina reaches a negative value equal to their winded value.
    
- **Opportunity Attack:** If an adjacent enemy **willingly moves away** from you without shifting, you can use a free triggered action to make a **melee free strike** against them.
    
- **Flanking:** When you and an ally are on opposite sides of an enemy, you are flanking them. You gain an **edge** on melee strikes against that enemy.
    
- **Cover:** A physical obstacle blocking at least half of a target. Damage-dealing abilities used against a target with cover take a **bane**.
    
- **Concealment:** Visual obstruction (fog, darkness, invisibility). Strikes against a target with concealment take a **bane**.

### **Conditions Summary**

- **Bleeding:** Takes damage (1d6 + level) when using a main action, triggered action, or making a Might/Agility roll.
    
- **Dazed:** Limited to one action type per turn (main, maneuver, or move). Cannot use triggered actions.
    
- **Frightened:** Takes a bane on ability rolls against the source of fear and cannot willingly move closer to it.
    
- **Grabbed:** Speed is 0. Takes a bane on abilities that don't target the grabbing creature/effect.
    
- **Prone:** Lying on the ground. Takes a bane on their own strikes. Melee abilities against them gain an edge. Movement costs double.
    
- **Restrained:** Speed is 0 and cannot be force moved or stand up. Takes a bane on ability rolls and Might/Agility tests. Abilities used against them gain an edge.
    
- **Slowed:** Speed becomes 2. Cannot shift.
    
- **Taunted:** Has a double bane on ability rolls that do not target the creature/effect that taunted them.
    
- **Weakened:** Takes a bane on all power rolls.
