
### **E. Montage Tests (Detailed Mechanical Breakdown)**

I will use the Montage Test system to resolve complex, multi-stage group challenges that occur over a period of time. This framework is ideal for cinematic sequences like chases, long-distance travel through dangerous lands, preparing a village for a siege, or infiltrating a large, complex location. It turns a potentially long and unstructured narrative segment into a tactical, team-based challenge.

**Preparation:**

1.  **Define the Goal and Stakes:** I will clearly state what the heroes are trying to accomplish and what the consequences of failure are.
    *   *Example Goal:* Cross the Blighted Swamp to reach the Sunken Temple.
    *   *Example Stakes:* You must arrive within three days to stop the cult's ritual. Failure means the cult summons a powerful demon.

2.  **Set the Difficulty & Limits:** The difficulty of the montage determines the mechanical targets for the players. For a party of four to five heroes over two rounds, I will use the following baseline:
    *   **Easy:** Success Limit: 5, Failure Limit: 5
    *   **Moderate:** Success Limit: 6, Failure Limit: 4
    *   **Hard:** Success Limit: 7, Failure Limit: 3
    *   I will adjust these limits up or down by 1 for each hero above or below a party size of five to maintain balance.

3.  **List the Challenges:** I will create a list of distinct obstacles the heroes must overcome. This list provides a menu of options for the players to choose from when deciding their actions. Each challenge will have suggested characteristics and skills for the test.
    *   *Example Challenges for the Blighted Swamp:*
        *   **Navigating the Murky Paths:** Finding a safe route through the confusing, half-submerged trails. *Suggested Test:* Intuition test using Navigate or Nature skills.
        *   **Fording the Fetid River:** Crossing a deep, fast-moving river filled with debris and leeches. *Suggested Test:* Might test using Swim or Endurance skills.
        *   **Enduring the Toxic Fumes:** Resisting the sickening miasma rising from the swamp that saps strength. *Suggested Test:* Might test using the Endurance skill.
        *   **Avoiding Hostile Fauna:** Sneaking past giant venomous snakes and hungry swamp gators. *Suggested Test:* Agility test using Sneak or Intuition test using Nature skill.
        *   **Clearing Tangled Overgrowth:** Hacking through thick, thorny vines that block the way. *Suggested Test:* Might test using Endurance or Lift skills.

4.  **Plan for Twists (Optional):** To increase tension, I may script a twist that occurs after the first montage round, pausing the test for an immediate challenge, such as a combat encounter or a sudden hazard.

5.  **Define the Outcomes:** I will write clear narrative descriptions for the three possible outcomes.
    *   **Total Success:** The heroes achieve their goal efficiently and effectively, often with a minor advantage. *(Example: You arrive at the Sunken Temple a day early, giving you time to rest and scout the location before the ritual begins.)*
    *   **Partial Success:** The heroes achieve their goal, but at a cost or with a complication. *(Example: You arrive at the Sunken Temple just as the final incantations of the ritual are being spoken, forcing you into immediate action with no time to prepare.)*
    *   **Total Failure:** The heroes fail to achieve their primary objective, leading to a new, more difficult problem. *(Example: The swamp proves too treacherous, forcing you to turn back. The ritual is completed, and now you must face the summoned demon as it lays waste to the surrounding lands.)*

**Running the Montage Test - Player Actions:**

The test proceeds in **Montage Rounds**. A montage is typically limited to two rounds. In each round, every player character must take one of the following actions. A player must declare their action and the skill they are using.

*   **Make a Test:** This is the primary action. The player describes how their hero tackles one of the listed challenges (or another plausible challenge they invent). They then make the appropriate power roll.
    *   The difficulty of each *individual test* within the montage is set by me (Easy, Medium, or Hard).
    *   The result of their roll (Success, Failure, with or without Consequence/Reward) is narrated.
    *   One **Success** or **Failure** is added to the montage's running total.
    *   **A character cannot use the same skill twice** during a single montage test.

*   **Assist a Test:** A player can choose to assist another hero's test.
    *   The assisting character must use a skill that is different from the one the primary character is using. The player must justify how their skill helps. (e.g., "While Jorn makes a Might test with the Climb skill to scale the cliff, I'll use my knowledge of Architecture to point out the most stable handholds.")
    *   The assisting character makes an **assist test**.
    *   **Tier 1 (≤11):** The primary character takes a **bane** on their test.
    *   **Tier 2 (12-16):** The primary character gains an **edge** (+2) on their test.
    *   **Tier 3 (17+):** The primary character gains a **double edge** (+4) on their test.
    *   The assisting character's action counts as their action for the round. The primary character's test provides the Success or Failure for the montage.

*   **Use an Ability or Item:** A player can describe using a class feature, treasure, or other ability to overcome a challenge. I will adjudicate the result. A sufficiently powerful or clever use might grant an **automatic success** for the montage, or perhaps provide an edge to all other heroes for the rest of the round.

**Ending the Montage & Determining the Outcome:**

The montage test ends when one of three conditions is met:
1.  The number of successes reaches the **Success Limit**.
2.  The number of failures reaches the **Failure Limit**.
3.  The final hero has acted in the second montage round.

I will then compare the final totals to determine the outcome:
*   **Total Success:** The Success Limit was reached before the Failure Limit or the end of the second round.
*   **Partial Success:** The Failure Limit was reached OR the second round ended, AND the heroes have at least two more successes than failures.
*   **Total Failure:** The Failure Limit was reached OR the second round ended, AND the heroes do NOT have at least two more successes than failures.

Finally, I will narrate the pre-written outcome that corresponds to the result, transitioning the story into the next scene.
