### **Group 1: The Core Fantasy Concepts**

#### **Golemforged Colossus "BERGRISI"**
*A nigh-unstoppable siege engine of granite and runes.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+3** | MOBILITY **-1** | SYSTEMS **+0**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 8 | Reactor: 3 | Harm: 4 (Melee)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Groundbreaker (1 Reactor):** When you **Engage a Foe** in melee, you can slam the ground instead of striking them. All enemy mechs engaged in close combat with you must make a MOBILITY roll or be thrown off-balance, unable to act for a moment.
*   **Siege Shield (Passive):** You are too bulky to dodge effectively. You cannot use the **Maneuver for Advantage** move to evade. However, when you **Brace for Impact**, you can choose to also protect one adjacent ally, who also benefits from your roll.
*   **Rune of Unmaking:** **[Requires: Master in Crafting]** As your last act, you can overload the heartgem. This is a GM move, but it will level the immediate area, guaranteeing destruction for you and anyone nearby.

#### **Wyrm-Scale Vanguard "LINDWURM"**
*A sleek and agile hunter-killer built for speed and surgical strikes.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+1** | MOBILITY **+3** | SYSTEMS **+1**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 5 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 2 (Claws/Breath)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Elemental Breath (1 Reactor):** Unleash your draco-aether engine as a 30ft cone of energy. All targets in the area must roll MOBILITY or take this mecha's Harm.
*   **Vibro-Claws (Passive):** Your melee attacks are keen enough to bypass armor. When you deal Harm with a melee attack, you can choose to deal 1 less Harm to instead target and damage a specific enemy system.
*   **Slippery Scales:** **[Requires: Expert in Acrobatics]** When an enemy succeeds on an **Engage a Foe** roll against you in melee, you can spend 1 Reactor to force them to re-roll their 2d6. They must take the second result.

#### **Sylvan Sentinel "SILVANUS"**
*A graceful and living machine, a fusion of primal magic and elven artistry.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+0** | MOBILITY **+2** | SYSTEMS **+3**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 6 | Reactor: 5 | Harm: 3 (Ranged)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Heartwood Mend (1 Reactor):** Divert auxiliary power to regrow damaged sections. Clear 2 boxes of marked Structure. You cannot perform any other action that turn.
*   **Entangling Shot (1 Reactor):** Your next ranged attack deals no Harm. Instead, it ensnares the target in thick, grasping vines, restraining them. They must waste their turn breaking free or suffer a -2 ongoing penalty to all actions.
*   **Genesis Arrow:** **[Requires: Master in Nature]** When you **Scan the Battlefield**, you can spend 2 Reactor to fire a perfect arrow of pure life energy. It deals no Harm. Instead, it restores an allied mecha's Structure to full.

#### **Abyssal Leviathan "KRAKEN"**
*A monstrous deep-sea warmachine built to crush and terrify.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+3** | MOBILITY **+0** | SYSTEMS **+1**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 7 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 4 (Crushing)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Crusher Claw (Passive):** If you successfully **Engage a Foe** with your pincer claw, you can choose to grapple them instead of dealing Harm. While grappled, they cannot move, and you can automatically deal your Harm to them as a free action on your turn.
*   **Abyssal Pressure Wave (2 Reactor):** You vent your core in a concussive blast. All mechs within close range are violently shoved away from you and must make a POWER roll to avoid being knocked prone.
*   **Bioluminescence:** **[Requires: Trained in Deception]** Spend 1 Reactor to flash a hypnotic light pattern. A single enemy pilot must make a Will save (vs your pilot's class DC) or be Fascinated for a round, unable to act.

#### **Celestial Judicator "AEGIS"**
*A divine engine of justice, seemingly forged from starlight.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+2** | MOBILITY **+1** | SYSTEMS **+2**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 6 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 3 (Holy Lance)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Wings of Hard Light (1 Reactor):** For one scene, you gain the ability to fly.
*   **Sanctified Ground (1 Reactor):** Your lance projects a shimmering dome of energy until your next turn. Any non-allied mech starting its turn inside the dome suffers 1 Harm, ignoring armor.
*   **Orbital Strike:** **[Requires: Master in Religion]** Once per battle, you can use your turn to pray and target a location. At the start of your next turn, a brilliant beam of holy light strikes that spot. It is a GM Move to determine the full effect, but it will be devastating.

### **Group 2: The "Metal Gear" Inspired**

#### **Grave Warden "KERBEROS"**
*A black-ops infiltration unit blending necromancy and tactical stealth.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+1** | MOBILITY **+2** | SYSTEMS **+3**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 5 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 3 (Sniper)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Stygian Leech (1 Reactor):** When you deal Harm to an enemy, you siphon their anima. Restore 1 of your own Structure.
*   **Shadow Veil (Passive):** When you are in shadow or darkness, enemies take a -2 penalty when trying to **Scan the Battlefield** to detect you.
*   **Puppet String:** **[Requires: Expert in Occultism]** Spend 2 Reactor to fire a reanimation bolt into a wrecked, unmanned mecha. For the next three rounds, it rises and fights for you as a simple drone before crumbling.

#### **Mycelian Saboteur "MYCONID"**
*A bizarre bio-weapon for guerrilla warfare and area denial.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+0** | MOBILITY **+1** | SYSTEMS **+2**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 6 | Reactor: 5 | Harm: 1 (Impact)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Launch Spore Pod (1 Reactor):** Fire a pod that blooms on impact. Choose one effect: create a dense smokescreen, an area of corrosive acid that deals 2 Harm to anyone entering it, or a hallucinogenic mist that confounds sensors.
*   **Fungal Camouflage (Passive):** If you spend a turn remaining perfectly still in a natural environment, you become completely hidden from sight and sensors until you move again.
*   **Symbiotic Repair:** **[Requires: Trained in Medicine or Nature]** You may spend 1 Reactor and a full turn to clear one permanent **DAMAGE** track box, as the fungus repairs critical systems. You cannot do this again for the rest of the battle.

#### **Dwarven Earthbreaker "JÖRMUNGANDR"**
*A segmented, mobile artillery platform that can tunnel through solid rock.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+2** | MOBILITY **-1** | SYSTEMS **+2**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 9 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 4 (Mortars)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Burrow (1 Reactor):** You can spend a turn to tunnel underground, vanishing from sight. You can reappear anywhere on the battlefield on your next turn as a surprise attack, gaining a +1 bonus on your next **Engage a Foe** roll.
*   **Siege Platform (Passive):** You cannot use the **Brace for Impact** move. However, you have Heavy Armor; all Structure damage you take from ranged attacks is reduced by 1.
*   **Runic Targeting:** **[Requires: Expert in Crafting]** Spend 1 Reactor to mark a single target. Your next Harm-dealing attack against that target cannot miss.

#### **Vampiric Nightwing "CAMAZOTZ"**
*A nocturnal aerial superiority unit that repairs itself by draining enemy wreckage.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+1** | MOBILITY **+3** | SYSTEMS **+1**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 4 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 2 (Ranged)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Sanguine Engine (Passive):** You have a swarm of metallic nanites. Anytime an enemy mecha is wrecked within sight, your nanites swarm its corpse. Restore 2 Structure.
*   **Screech Cannon (1 Reactor):** Unleash a high-frequency acoustic assault. Pick a single target. They suffer a -2 penalty on all SYSTEMS rolls for the rest of the scene.
*   **Aerial Ace:** **[Requires: Expert in Piloting Lore or Acrobatics]** When you **Maneuver for Advantage** to dodge, on a 10+, you not only get to a safe position, but you can also immediately take another action.

#### **Hydra-Class War Platform "LERNIA"**
*A colossal, multi-headed command unit bristling with weapon systems.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+3** | MOBILITY **+0** | SYSTEMS **+3**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 10 | Reactor: 6 | Harm: 3 (Multi-weapon)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Alpha Strike (2 Reactor):** Fire three of your weapon-heads at once. Make a single **Engage a Foe** roll against up to three different targets.
*   **Autonomous Heads (Passive):** This mecha is too complex to easily disable. The first time in a battle that you would take **DAMAGE**, you can choose to ignore it. One of your non-command weapon heads is destroyed instead.
*   **Command Network:** **[Requires: Trained in Diplomacy or Society]** Spend 1 Reactor to broadcast tactical data. All allied mechs gain a +1 bonus on their next **Scan the Battlefield** or **Engage a Foe** roll.

### **Group 3: The Unique & Non-Humanoid**

#### **Arachnid Foundry "HEPHAESTUS"**
*A mobile spider-like workshop that can repair allies in the heat of battle.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+1** | MOBILITY **+0** | SYSTEMS **+3**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 7 | Reactor: 5 | Harm: 1 (Tools)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Field Repair (1 Reactor):** Touch an adjacent allied mecha. They immediately restore 3 Structure. You cannot use this on yourself.
*   **Forge Armor (1 Reactor):** Touch an adjacent allied mecha. For the rest of the scene, they gain 1-point of armor, reducing all incoming Structure damage by 1.
*   **Fabricate Weapon:** **[Requires: Master in Crafting]** Spend 2 Reactor and a full turn. You can replace an allied mecha's destroyed weapon system with a crude but functional temporary replacement.

#### **Siege Roller "GOLIATH"**
*A terrifying and simple weapon: a colossal, heavily armored, rolling engine of destruction.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+4** | MOBILITY **+0** | SYSTEMS **-1**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 8 | Reactor: 2 | Harm: 5 (Crushing)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Momentum (Passive):** You cannot stop easily. Reversing your direction or making a sharp turn requires a full turn's action. If you successfully **Maneuver for Advantage** to move in a straight line, deal your Harm to anything you roll over.
*   **Unstoppable (Passive):** You cannot be grappled, pushed, or restrained. You can roll directly through terrain that would block other mechs, though the GM might have you shred Structure when doing so.

#### **High-Altitude Platform "MANTA"**
*A graceful, high-altitude surveillance and communications relay.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **-1** | MOBILITY **+2** | SYSTEMS **+4**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 4 | Reactor: 5 | Harm: 1 (Pulse)
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Sensor Ghost (1 Reactor):** You fade from all enemy sensors. For the rest of the scene, any action made to target you by a non-visual system automatically fails.
*   **Network Hub (Passive):** You see everything. When an ally makes a **Scan the Battlefield** roll, they can use your SYSTEMS stat instead of their own.
*   **Long-Range Scry:** **[Requires: Trained in Arcana or Survival]** You can extend your senses far beyond the battlefield. You can use **Scan the Battlefield** to ask questions about locations, troop movements, or threats that are miles away.

#### **Combat Chariot "BOUDICA"**
*A unique command unit: a central cockpit flanked by two bestial combat drones.*
---
**STATS:** POWER **+2** | MOBILITY **+2** | SYSTEMS **+1**
**RESOURCES:** Structure: 6 | Reactor: 4 | Harm: 3
---
**MECH MOVES:**
*   **Coordinated Strike (1 Reactor):** Command your drones to flank a target. Your next **Engage a Foe** roll against that target gets a +2 bonus and deals +1 Harm.
*   **Decouple (Passive):** The first time your Structure track is completely filled, you do not take DAMAGE. Instead, one of your drones is destroyed. Your POWER is now -1 until repaired. If this happens a second time, your other drone is destroyed.
*   **Energy Reins:** **[Requires: Trained in Animal Handling Lore or Acrobatics]** Spend 1 Reactor to perform a "slingshot" maneuver. Use your drones to violently propel your chariot. Automatically succeed at a **Maneuver for Advantage** roll to get into or out of any situation.
