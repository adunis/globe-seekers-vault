 # Zorvath's Mecha: The Chronos Engine

Zorvath, the enigmatic Netherese mage, operates on a different scale than the gods. While the deities focus on preserving their followers and establishing new domains, Zorvath's ambition transcends mere survival. His mecha, the "Chronos Engine," is not designed for direct combat or colonization in the traditional sense, but as a tool for manipulating time and space, a means to achieve his ultimate goal: the restoration of the Netherese Empire to its former glory, perhaps even by rewriting history itself.

## Core Concepts:

*   **Temporal Manipulation:** The Chronos Engine is less a war machine and more a mobile temporal displacement unit, capable of localized time dilation, temporal jumps, and even limited reality manipulation.
*   **N-Shard Integration:** Unlike the divine mechas that primarily use divine essence, the Chronos Engine is heavily integrated with N-Shards, fragments of Netherese magic that grant it unparalleled control over arcane energies and temporal mechanics.
*   **Stealth and Subtlety:** Zorvath prefers to operate from the shadows, and his mecha reflects this. It is designed for infiltration, observation, and precise, surgical strikes rather than brute force.

## The Chronos Engine:

*   **Description:** The Chronos Engine is a sleek, obsidian-black mecha with glowing, intricate runic patterns etched across its surface. It appears almost ethereal, shimmering slightly as if constantly phasing in and out of reality. It has no obvious weapons, its power lying in its ability to distort the flow of time and space around it.
*   **Abilities:**
    *   **Temporal Displacement:** Can jump short distances through time, appearing and disappearing instantaneously.
    *   **Time Dilation Field:** Can create localized fields that slow down or speed up time for those within them.
    *   **Reality Weave Manipulation:** Can subtly alter the fabric of reality, creating illusions, reinforcing structures, or even temporarily negating magical effects.
    *   **N-Shard Siphon:** Can absorb and process N-Shard energy, allowing it to power its advanced temporal and arcane systems.
    *   **Chronal Anchor:** Can establish temporary anchors in the timeline, allowing Zorvath to observe or intervene in past or future events without fully committing to a temporal jump.

## Zorvath's Objectives with the Chronos Engine:

1.  **Gathering Netherese Artifacts:** The mecha allows Zorvath to locate and retrieve lost Netherese artifacts across the galaxy, regardless of their temporal location.
2.  **Observing and Manipulating Timelines:** Zorvath uses the Chronos Engine to observe key historical events and subtly manipulate them to his advantage, aiming to prevent the fall of Netheril or to ensure its resurgence.
3.  **Countering Divine Influence:** The Chronos Engine can disrupt the divine mechas and their operations by manipulating their localized timelines or by creating temporal paradoxes that weaken their divine connection.
4.  **Seeking the True Weave:** Zorvath believes that the fractured Weave is a symptom of a deeper cosmic imbalance. He uses the Chronos Engine to search for the original, untainted source of magic, hoping to restore it and, in doing so, restore Netheril.
5.  **Confronting Ancient Foes:** The Chronos Engine allows Zorvath to directly confront entities that threaten his plans, even those that exist outside conventional time and space.

Zorvath and his Chronos Engine represent a unique and dangerous force in the new sci-fi fantasy setting, operating beyond the conventional conflicts of gods and mortals, with a singular, time-bending agenda.
