# Transition from Globeseekers 2 to Globeseekers 3: The Dawn of the God-Fellers

As the campaign of Globeseekers 2 draws to its climax, the heroes find themselves embroiled in the sinister machinations of Vecna. Their current mission has led them deep into the Duergar laboratories, hidden within the desolate landscapes under Vecna's control. What they are about to uncover will not only redefine their understanding of the ongoing cosmic conflict but also serve as the direct catalyst for the Great Exodus and the dawn of the God-Fellers.

## The Revelation in Vecna's Labs:

Currently, the party is investigating the Duergar laboratories, which are under the direct influence and control of Vecna. These labs are rumored to be involved in grotesque experiments and the creation of powerful constructs. As the party delves deeper, they will stumble upon a horrifying secret:

*   **Discovery of the First God-Feller:** Within the deepest, most heavily guarded sections of the Duergar labs, the party will discover a colossal, partially constructed mecha. This is not just any construct; it is the prototype of what will come to be known as a "God-Feller." Its design is clearly intended for combat on a scale far beyond mortal understanding, and its core is being integrated with raw, unstable N-Shards.
*   **Vecna's Ambition:** Through intercepted communications, schematics, or the interrogation of Duergar engineers, the party will learn that Vecna intends to use these God-Fellers to assert his dominance over the remaining divine powers and potentially even challenge the imprisoned New Gods. The N-Shards are crucial to this, as they provide the raw power necessary to combat divine essence.

## The Secret Arms Race: Before the Exodus

Unbeknownst to the mortal realms, a desperate and secret arms race has been underway among the gods themselves, and even by Zorvath. The fracturing of the Weave and the weakening of divine influence have led many deities to a terrifying conclusion: direct divine intervention is no longer sufficient. To survive the encroaching chaos and to secure their future, they need a new kind of weapon – one capable of directly engaging and even destroying god-avatars.

*   **Divine Paranoia:** Each god, sensing the growing instability and the ambitions of their rivals, began to secretly commission or construct their own mechas, designed specifically to combat other divine beings. The N-Shards, fragments of raw magical power, became the coveted resource for these projects.
*   **Zorvath's Foresight:** Zorvath, with his ancient Netherese knowledge and temporal insights, foresaw this inevitable escalation. He too began developing his own unique mecha, the "Chronos Engine," not necessarily to kill god-avatars in direct combat, but to manipulate the very timeline of this divine conflict to his advantage, perhaps even to prevent the complete destruction of Faerûn or to ensure Netheril's resurgence.
*   **The "God-Feller" Designation:** The term "God-Feller" (or "God-Slayer" in some ancient texts) is an internal designation used by those aware of these projects, referring to mechas specifically designed and empowered by N-Shards to combat and defeat divine manifestations or even lesser deities.

## The Catalyst for Exodus:

The discovery of Vecna's God-Feller project will be the final, undeniable proof that Faerûn is doomed. The realization that even the gods are resorting to such desperate measures, building weapons to kill each other, will underscore the severity of the situation. This revelation, combined with the escalating planar incursions and the continued decay of the Weave, will force the remaining factions to accelerate their plans for the Great Exodus.

*   **Urgency:** The party's discovery will trigger a heightened sense of urgency among the surviving leaders. The race to escape Faerûn will become paramount.
*   **New Alliances/Conflicts:** The knowledge of God-Fellers will reshape alliances and rivalries. Some factions might seek to steal Vecna's designs, while others might redouble their efforts to complete their own mechas.
*   **The Journey Begins:** The campaign will conclude with the party either participating in the final desperate push for the Exodus, or being among the first to embark on the perilous journey into the cosmos, leaving behind a dying world and heading towards the unknown challenges of Globeseekers 3.

This transition sets the stage for a campaign where the remnants of a fantasy world clash in a sci-fi setting, armed with ancient magic and newly forged, god-killing machines.
