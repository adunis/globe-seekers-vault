A black-ops infiltration unit blending necromantic technology and tactical stealth for silent eliminations.

> **STATS & RESOURCES**  
> **POWER:** +1 | **MOBILITY:** +2 | **SYSTEMS:** +3

    - **Harm:** 4 | **Tags:** Ranged (Extreme), Armor Piercing, Precise
        
    - **Description:** Electromagnetically launches a sub-caliber, cryo-coated slug that chills and shatters armor plating on impact.
        
    - **System & Loadout:** Manual Bolt-Action. **Magazine:** 1. **Carried:** 6 HVAP rounds. The streamlined design imposes no mobility malus.
        
2. **K-DART 'Leech' System**
    
    - **Harm:** 2 | **Tags:** Melee, Thrown (Close), Siphoning
        
    - **Description:** Fires or throws darts containing anima-reactive nanites that break down enemy armor to repair the user's own structure.
        
    - **System & Loadout:** Wrist-Mounted Launcher. **Magazine:** 3 darts. **Carried:** 2 magazines. No mobility malus.
        
3. **'Spectre' Psycho-Acoustic Mine**
    
    - **Harm:** 1 | **Tags:** Ranged (Thrown), Area, Fear, Lingering
        
    - **Description:** A deployable mine that releases a low-frequency signal, causing hallucinations and system glitches in enemy sensor suites.
        
    - **System & Loadout:** Throwable Device. **Carried:** 3 mines. No mobility malus.
        

### EQUIPMENT & TACTICAL SYSTEMS

1. **'Stygian' Siphon Module**
    
    - **Move:** When you **damage an enemy and activate the siphon**, spend 1 Reactor. The module tears away vital energy along with armor, restoring 1 of your own Structure.
        
2. **'Hades' Stealth System**
    
    - **Move:** When you **conceal yourself in shadow or darkness**, the light-dampening field activates. Anyone using **Scan the Battlefield** to detect you takes a -2 penalty.
        
3. **'Thanatos' Necrotech Bolt**
    
    - **Move:** When you **fire a reanimation bolt into a wrecked, unmanned mecha**, spend 2 Reactor. If you have **Expert in Occultism**, the bolt injects a viral machine-spirit. For the next three rounds, the wreck rises and fights for you as a simple drone before crumbling.

    - **Harm:** 2 | **Tags:** Ranged (Medium)
