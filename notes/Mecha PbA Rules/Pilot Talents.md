### **Core Principle: The Pilot Talent**

Your Pilot Talent is a unique passive ability or a special action you can take, reflecting your core class training. It applies to any mecha you pilot. Some talents are subtle, while others are dramatic.

---

### **Warrior Classes**

#### **Fighter: Peerless Combatant**

Your training translates to unmatched precision in mecha combat.

- **[Press the Advantage]:** When you succeed (10+) on an **Engage a Foe** roll, you can immediately make another attack against the same target at a -2 penalty (roll 2d6+POWER-2). You cannot use this follow-up attack to trigger this talent again.
    
- **Subclass - Open Hand:** **[Pushing Assault]:** When you hit an enemy with a melee attack, you can also choose to move them, shoving them into a bad position or into another enemy.
    
- **Subclass - Two-Handed Grip:** **[Power Strike]:** When you **Engage a Foe** with a heavy melee weapon, you can declare you are over-swinging. Treat the mecha's Harm as +2 for this attack, but you also suffer 1 Structure damage from the feedback, hit or miss.
    

#### **Barbarian: Uncontrolled Fury**

Your rage becomes the mecha's own, pushing its systems to their absolute limit.

- **[Enter the Rage]:** Once per battle, you can push your mecha's reactor into the red. For the rest of the scene, gain +1 to POWER, but take a -1 to MOBILITY. When you exit this state, shred 2 Structure from system strain.
    
- **Instinct - Animal:** **[Bestial Charge]:** When you **Maneuver for Advantage** to charge an enemy, on a 10+, you also deal half your mecha's Harm as you collide with them.
    
- **Instinct - Spirit:** **[Ghost in the Machine]:** Once per battle, describe how you channel spectral energy. For one moment, you can pass through a solid object or ignore another mecha's physical armor to deal damage directly to their Structure. This costs 2 Reactor.
    
- **Instinct - Giant:** **[Hurl Wreckage]:** You can pick up and throw destroyed mechs, large boulders, or other massive objects as a weapon. This always counts as an **Engage a Foe** roll using POWER.
    

#### **Monk: Mecha as an Extension of Self**

You treat the mecha's frame as your own body, finding a unique and dangerous balance.

- **[Mecha Stance]:** At the start of a battle, choose a stance. You can switch as a turn's action.
    
    - Iron Temple Stance: Gain +1 to **Brace for Impact** rolls.
        
    - Crane Wing Stance: Gain +1 to **Maneuver for Advantage** rolls when dodging.
        
    - Tiger Claw Stance: Your unarmed mecha strikes (punches, kicks) deal +1 Harm.
        
- **[Flurry of Blows]:** Once per battle, when you **Engage a Foe** with an unarmed mecha attack and hit (7+), you can immediately make a second unarmed strike that deals half Harm.

### **Divine Classes**

#### **Champion: The Unbreakable Bastion**

Your divine conviction turns your machine into a symbol of hope or dread.

- **Cause - Paladin:** **[Retributive Strike]:** When an enemy mecha hits an allied mecha near you, your next **Engage a Foe** roll against that enemy gains a +1 bonus.
    
- **Cause - Redeemer:** **[Disarming Rebuke]:** Once per battle, when you **Engage a Foe**, you can choose to deal no Harm on a hit. Instead, you target their primary weapon system, rendering it inoperable until they spend a full turn to reset it.
    
- **Cause - Liberator:** **[For Freedom!]:** You excel at freeing others. When you act to remove an obstacle pinning an ally (physical debris, an energy net, a grapple), you do not need to roll. It just works.
    

#### **Cleric: Divine Conduit**

You channel your deity's power through the mecha's arcane systems.

- **[Channel Divinity]:** You can use your pilot's divine font to affect other machines.
    
    - Heal: By touching an allied mecha, you can expend one use of your Divine Font to restore 3 Structure.
        
    - Harm: By making a touch attack (requires an **Engage a Foe** roll), you can expend one use of your Divine Font to disrupt their systems, inflicting 2 Harm that ignores armor.
        
- **Doctrine - Warpriest:** You are trained for the front lines. Add half your proficiency bonus in Athletics (rounded down) to your mecha's POWER.
    
- **Doctrine - Cloistered:** You are a conduit for divine knowledge. When you **Scan the Battlefield**, on a 7-9 you can still ask two questions instead of one.
    

#### **Druid: Primal Synthesis**

You don't just pilot the machine; you merge its functions with the natural world around it.

- **[Wild Shape Integration]:** Once per battle, you can temporarily reshape your mecha's form using primal energy, choosing one:
    
    - Savage Form: Your melee weapons grow vicious thorns and claws. Deal +1 Harm in melee for the scene.
        
    - Aerial Form: Sprout temporary wings of hard light. You can fly clumsily for the scene.
        
    - Earth Form: Your legs merge into the ground. You cannot move, but gain +2 on **Brace for Impact** rolls.
        
- **Order - Storm:** You can try to manipulate the weather itself. This is always a **Scan the Battlefield** roll, where a success could create a blinding fog, a dense downpour, or call a single lightning strike.
    
- **Order - Leaf:** You can entangle foes. Spend 1 Reactor to cause vines or metallic cables to erupt from the ground, restraining an enemy mecha. They must waste their turn breaking free.

### **Arcane Classes**

#### **Wizard: The Arcane Technician**

Your scholarly knowledge allows you to manipulate and overclock your mecha's magical systems.

- **[Overchannel Systems]:** When you trigger a **Mech Move** (your mecha's unique abilities), you can expend one of your PF2e spell slots. If you do, you don't spend a Reactor point, and the move's effect is magnified (GM's discretion - more Harm, larger area, longer duration). The higher the spell slot, the greater the effect.
    
- **School - Evocation:** **[Sculpt Energy]:** When you use an Area of Effect energy weapon (flamethrower, lightning arc), you can exclude one ally from the target area so they take no damage.
    
- **School - Transmutation:** **[System Reconfiguration]:** Once per battle, you can spend ten minutes re-routing power. Move 1 point from one Mecha Stat to another for the rest of the day (e.g., reduce MOBILITY by 1 to increase POWER by 1).
    

#### **Sorcerer: Bloodline Overload**

Your innate magic surges through the mecha's conduits, with powerful and unpredictable results.

- **Bloodline - Draconic:** **[Elemental Breath]:** Spend 1 Reactor. Your mecha unleashes a 30ft cone of energy matching your draconic ancestor. All mechs caught inside must make a MOBILITY roll or take 3 Harm.
    
- **Bloodline - Angelic:** **[Halo Shield]:** Spend 1 Reactor to create a halo of divine energy. For the next three rounds, all allied mechs adjacent to you take 1 less Structure damage from all attacks.
    
- **Bloodline - Imperial:** **[Arcane Pulse]:** Spend 1 Reactor to send out a pulse of pure magic. Temporarily nullify the special defenses (stealth fields, magic shields) of all enemies within visual range for one round.
    

#### **Magus: The Spell-Striker**

You channel your magic directly through your mecha's weapons for devastating combined attacks.

- **[Mecha Spellstrike]:** When you **Engage a Foe** with a melee weapon, you can expend one of your PF2e spell slots. If the attack hits (7+), you add the spell's effect to the strike in addition to the normal Harm. A Gouging Claw spell might shred their armor, while a Telekinetic Projectile spell adds extra impact damage.

#### **Alchemist: The Volatile Genius**

Your mecha is your laboratory, and combat is just a field experiment.

- **[Quick Alchemy]:** During combat, you can spend a full turn to quickly craft and apply one of the following to your mecha. This costs 1 Reactor.
    
    - Juggernaut Mutagen: Gain +1 POWER but -1 MOBILITY for the scene.
        
    - Quick-Clot Sealant: Restore 2 Structure.
        
    - Energy Bomb: Attach a thrown explosive to your mecha's arm. Your next ranged attack is an Area of Effect blast.
        

#### **Inventor: Unstable Overdrive**

You built this thing, you know its limits, and you know exactly how to break them.

- **[OVERDRIVE!]:** You can push your invention beyond its design specifications. When you do, gain one of these benefits for your next action: gain a +2 bonus to your roll, deal +3 Harm, or automatically succeed at a Maneuver roll. After the action, roll a d6. On a 1-3, your invention backfires spectacularly. The GM makes a hard move against you.
    

#### **Ranger: The Patient Hunter**

You are a master of singling out and dismantling a target.

- **[Hunt Target]:** At the start of the battle, designate a single enemy mecha as your prey. You gain a +1 bonus on all **Scan the Battlefield** rolls made concerning your prey. Furthermore, when you deal Harm to your prey, you deal +1 Harm. You can only have one prey at a time.
    

#### **Rogue: The Ghost in the Shell**

You use misdirection and precision strikes to dismantle foes.

- **[Debilitating Strike]:** When you succeed (10+) on an **Engage a Foe** roll against an enemy who is distracted or flanked, you can choose to trade your Harm. Instead of dealing Structure damage, you inflict a crippling status. Give the enemy -1 ongoing to their MOBILITY or POWER until they can repair.
    

#### **Swashbuckler: The Ace Pilot**

You pilot with daring, flair, and incredible panache.

- **[Panache]:** You have a special resource called **Panache** (a simple check box). You gain Panache when you succeed (10+) on a **Maneuver for Advantage** roll.
    
- **[Daring Finisher]:** When you have Panache, you can declare you are using a Finisher when you **Engage a Foe**. You lose Panache, but your attack gains a special property:
    
    - Precise Strike: Ignore 2 points of enemy armor/defenses.
        
    - Bleeding Finish: If you deal Harm, the enemy also takes 1 additional Harm at the start of their next turn from "bleeding" systems.
        

#### **Thaumaturge: Exploit Vulnerability**

You know that every machine, like every creature, has a secret weakness.

- **[Exploit Vulnerability]:** At the start of a fight, or by taking a turn to **Scan the Battlefield**, you can identify an esoteric weakness in a target mecha (e.g., "its unshielded power conduit," "a resonance flaw in its dwarven steel," "its psychic feedback loop"). All allies (including you) who target that specific vulnerability gain +1 Harm against that mecha.

### **Investigator: The Master Analyst**

Your mind moves faster than any machine, processing tactical data to find the perfect, irrefutable solution to any combat problem.

- **[Pursue a Lead]:** This talent grants you a unique resource: **A Lead**.
    
    - You gain **A Lead** on a specific enemy mecha whenever you successfully (7+) use the **Scan the Battlefield** move to gather information about them.
        
    - You can spend **A Lead** when you next **Engage that Foe** or **Maneuver for Advantage** against them. When you do, you have found the perfect angle of attack or predicted their exact path; roll using your mecha's **SYSTEMS** stat instead of its POWER or MOBILITY. Your intellect overcomes their physical prowess. You can only have one Lead at a time.
        
    
    **Why it fits:** This talent directly ports the Investigator's core gameplay loop—Devise a Stratagem—into the PbtA framework. It’s about gaining knowledge first (Scanning) and then using that knowledge to guarantee a superior outcome (swapping to your best stat). It rewards a thoughtful, analytical playstyle.

### **Witch: The Hextech Engineer**

You don't just pilot your mecha; you infest it. Your patron's otherworldly power seeps into the machine's wiring, turning it into a conduit for potent and debilitating curses.

- **[Patron's Hex]:** Your familiar integrates with your mecha, allowing you to deliver debilitating hexes through its systems.
    
    - When you successfully hit an enemy with an attack or target them with a successful **Scan the Battlefield** roll (7+), you can choose to spend **1 Reactor**.
        
    - If you do, you inflict a lingering **Hex** upon the target, chosen from the list below based on your Patron's theme. A mecha can only suffer from one of your Hexes at a time.
        
    
    **Choose Your Hexes (Examples):**
    
    - **Curse/Resentment:** **[Feedback Loop]:** The next time the hexed pilot attempts any action (hit or miss), their own systems revolt, inflicting 1 automatic Structure damage to their mecha.
        
    - **Night/Shadow:** **[Sensor Scramble]:** Ghostly shadows and false readings plague their sensors. The target takes a -2 penalty to their next **Scan the Battlefield** roll, and you are considered hidden from them until you attack again.
        
    - **Fate/Rune:** **[Locked Plate]:** A rune of binding appears on their mecha's armor. The target cannot use the **Brace for Impact** move or other purely defensive actions until they waste a turn resetting their systems.
        
    - **Winter:** **[Rime-Coated Actuators]:** Hoarfrost, real or arcane, covers their mecha's joints. The target suffers a -2 penalty to their next **Maneuver for Advantage** roll.
        
    - **Wild:** **[Corrosive Spores]:** Your mecha vents a cloud of magically corrosive spores. The target's armor is compromised, and the next source of Harm they take from anyone deals +1 Harm.
        
    
    **Why it fits:** This talent captures the Witch's core identity as a debuffer and master of esoteric magic. By tying the Hexes to successful actions, it keeps the game flowing. The link to the Patron ensures that the player's core character choices in PF2e are directly reflected in their mecha's unique capabilities, making them feel like a true arcane saboteur on the battlefield.
