### **Core System: Mecha Override**

This system overlays PF2e when you enter your mecha. All combat is resolved using the PbtA framework below. The core principle is: **The Mecha is the tool, the Pilot is the skill.**

**The Core Mechanic:** When your pilot takes decisive action that fits one of the **Basic Moves**, you roll **2d6 + the relevant Mecha Stat**.

- **On a 10+ (Success):** You do it. You achieve your goal with skill and efficiency.
    
- **On a 7-9 (Mixed Success):** You succeed, but with a cost. The GM will choose a complication from the list: you take harm, you're put in a bad spot, you damage your own mecha, or you cause unintended collateral damage.
    
- **On a 6- (Miss):** Things go wrong. The GM makes a hard move, and you do not achieve your immediate goal. The situation spirals.
    

---

### **Mecha Stats & Resources**

Each mecha has a static profile. A BEHEMOTH is always a lumbering brute, regardless of who is inside.

- **POWER:** Raw strength, weapon payload, melee capability. (Typical Range: +0 to +3)
    
- **MOBILITY:** Speed, reflexes, stealth, fine manipulation. (Typical Range: -1 to +3)
    
- **SYSTEMS:** Sensors, hacking, arcane reactors, special equipment. (Typical Range: -1 to +3)
    
- **STRUCTURE:** Your mecha's integrity. Usually 6-8 boxes. Taking harm marks boxes. When all are marked, clear the track and take 1 point of **DAMAGE**.
    
- **DAMAGE:** Permanent critical damage. Usually 3 boxes. Each point incurs a major penalty (e.g., -1 to a stat, weapon destroyed, reactor leak). Marking the 3rd box means your mecha is wrecked.
    
- **REACTOR:** Energy for powerful, unique **Mech Moves**. Usually a pool of 3-5 points.

### **The Basic Moves (All Mechs)**

- **When you Engage a Foe directly (with guns, blades, or fists)...**  
    Roll +**POWER**. On a success, you deal this mecha's Harm. On a 7-9, you deal harm, but expose yourself to a counterattack, taking harm in return.
    
- **When you Maneuver for Advantage (to close the distance, flee, or get a better position)...**  
    Roll +**MOBILITY**. On a 10+, you get where you're going and the situation is to your advantage. On a 7-9, you get there, but you're either in a dangerous spot or had to leave yourself wide open to do it.
    
- **When you Scan the Battlefield (using sensors, magic, or your own sharp eyes)...**  
    Roll +**SYSTEMS**. On a 10+, ask the GM 2 questions. On a 7-9, ask 1.
    
    - Where is my best escape route / point of cover?
        
    - Which enemy is the biggest threat?
        
    - What here can I use to my advantage?
        
    - What is my enemy's current intention?
        
- **When you Brace for Impact...**  
    Roll +**POWER**. On a 10+, halve the incoming Structure damage. On a 7-9, you take the full brunt, but manage to protect your pilot and core systems from critical damage.
