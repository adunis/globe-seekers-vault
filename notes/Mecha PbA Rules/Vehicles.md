### **Vehicle Rules & Stat Blocks**

These vehicles aren't piloted with the PbtA system. They are treated as **hazards, obstacles, or background elements** for hero mecha to deal with, or as enhanced equipment for PF2e NPCs/PCs.

When targeting these, a single successful **Engage a Foe** roll from a full-sized mecha is usually enough to destroy one. The threat comes from their numbers.

For soldiers operating them, they provide **cover**, grant **new actions**, or offer a **pool of HP** before the operator is targeted.

---

### **1. The "Piglet" Light Walker**

Often called the "Scrap-Hog" or "Rivet-Pig," this goblin-designed bipedal scout walker is the cheapest, most common mech on any battlefield. It's barely armored and prone to catastrophic, explosive failure.

**Description:** A rickety, one-person bipedal walker standing about 10 feet tall. The cockpit is an open cage of welded iron bars, barely protecting the goblin, kobold, or human operator. One arm is a hydraulic claw for clearing debris, the other mounts a light, belt-fed bolt thrower. It sputters greasy, alchemical smoke.

**Military Role:** Reconnaissance, skirmishing, and acting as a disposable screen for more valuable assets. Their commanders expect a 70% casualty rate and plan accordingly.

**As Equipment (For a PC/NPC):**

- **HP:** 30; **Hardness:** 5
    
- **Operator Gains:** Cover.
    
- **Actions:**
    
    - **Claw Arm [One Action]:** Melee attack, 1d10 Bludgeoning damage. Can be used to clear debris.
        
    - **Bolt Thrower [One Action]:** Ranged attack (60ft), 1d8 Piercing damage.
        
- **Critical Failure:** On a fumble with an attack, or when destroyed, the alchemical engine explodes. The operator and anyone adjacent takes 3d6 Fire damage.

### **2. Dwarven "Aegis" Mobile Shield**

This vehicle is less an attacker and more a mobile piece of dwarven architecture. It's a testament to the idea that the best offense is a truly immovable defense.

**Description:** A wide, low-to-the-ground tracked vehicle, essentially a moving wall of rune-etched granite and steel. It has no main weapons. Firing slits on the front allow up to three soldiers behind it to shoot their personal weapons in near-perfect safety. A single driver operates it from a cramped, protected hatch.

**Military Role:** Providing mobile cover for infantry advances, blocking streets, and establishing rapid fortifications. Often deployed in groups of three to create a solid wall.

**As Equipment (For a PC/NPC):**

- **HP:** 60; **Hardness:** 12
    
- **Provides:** Greater Cover to up to 4 Medium creatures behind it.
    
- **Actions:**
    
    - **Rune of Warding [One Action] (Driver Only):** The driver can trace a rune. For one round, the shield's Hardness becomes 18. This can't be used again for 1d4 rounds.
        
    - **Shield Bash [Two Actions] (Driver Only):** The vehicle lurches forward. All creatures in a 10-foot line must make a DC 22 Reflex save or be knocked prone and take 2d8 Bludgeoning damage.

### **3. Elven "Weaver" Skimmer**

A silent, grav-propelled artillery platform. It hovers about a foot off the ground, moving with eerie grace. It exemplifies the elven doctrine of striking with precision from unexpected angles, then vanishing.

**Description:** A sleek, crescent-shaped skimmer made of a polished, wood-like material. It is crewed by two elves: a pilot and a gunner. The gunner operates a "ballista" that fires solidified bolts of pure magic, which are "woven" in a crystal matrix before launch.

**Military Role:** Tank hunting and long-range fire support. They are fragile but excel at hit-and-run tactics, firing a single devastating shot before repositioning.

**As Equipment (For a PC/NPC):**

- **HP:** 25; **Hardness:** 3
    
- **Special:** This vehicle has a **Fly** speed of 50ft, but must remain within 5ft of the ground.
    
- **Actions (Gunner):**
    
    - **Weave Bolt [Three Actions]:** Ranged attack (200ft range increment), 3d12 Force damage. The slow loading process makes it difficult to fire more than once every other round.
        
- **Silent Running:** The skimmer is almost impossible to hear. Any creature trying to detect it using Perception based on sound has the DC increased by 10.

### **4. "Cauldron" Alchemical Mortar Carrier**

A strange, tri-legged walker operated by a single soldier, typically an alchemist or engineer. Its main feature is a large, bubbling vat of alchemical slurry that can be lobbed in horrifying concoctions.

**Description:** Standing 12 feet tall, this walker is more of a mobile weapon platform than a true vehicle. Its three legs give it stability on uneven ground. The operator stands on a small platform behind the mortar-vat, protected by a high blast shield.

**Military Role:** Area denial and suppressing infantry. Invaluable for breaking up formations and making terrain hazardous.

**As Equipment (For a PC/NPC):**

- **HP:** 20; **Hardness:** 4
    
- **Operator Gains:** Cover (from the front).
    
- **Actions (Operator):**
    
    - **Lob Mortar [Two Actions]:** Choose a target square within 100ft. All creatures in a 10-foot burst from that square must make a save based on the loaded alchemical mixture (typically DC 20). The operator must spend an action between shots to load a new mixture.
        
        - **Slurry Type A (Sticky Goo):** Reflex save or be Immobilized until they spend an action to escape.
            
        - **Slurry Type B (Acidic Slime):** Reflex save or take 4d6 persistent Acid damage.
            
        - **Slurry Type C (Concussive Gel):** Fortitude save or be Stunned 1.

### **5. Knightly Order "Lancer" Charger**

A single-pilot cavalry "mech" that looks like a fusion of a warhorse and a motorcycle. It is favored by the knightly orders of metropolitan kingdoms for urban pacification and shock charges.

**Description:** A mechanical quadruped chassis with a sleek, armored design that evokes the barding of a warhorse. The rider sits atop it like a motorcycle, legs protected by armored plating. Its primary weapon is a powerful, disposable Arc-Lance mounted to the front.

**Military Role:** Line-breaking, anti-vehicle charges, and rapid response.

**As Equipment (For a PC/NPC):**

- **HP:** 40; **Hardness:** 8
    
- **Special:** Has a land speed of 60ft.
    
- **Actions:**
    
    - **Charge Lance [Three Actions]:** The Charger moves up to its speed in a straight line and makes a melee strike against a single target at the end of its movement. On a hit, the lance shatters, dealing 6d8 Piercing damage and the target is pushed back 10 feet. The lance is destroyed. Equipping a new lance takes one minute.
        
    - **Foreleg Stomp [One Action]:** Melee attack, 1d12 Bludgeoning damage. Can be used while a lance is equipped.
        
- **Jousting Saddle:** A rider with the Cavalier Dedication can use their Mighty Charge feat while piloting the Lancer.
